Common questions

Before you write

Where are my photographs?

Inside Lupo, until you choose to save them. Saving writes them into your own Photos library, into an album of their own.

Anything you haven't saved is lost if you delete Lupo. The app keeps your work in its own storage, and deleting the app deletes that storage. Save anything you want to keep.

What data does Lupo collect?

No name, no email, no account, no location, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other apps.

Your photographs never leave your device. The only thing that goes anywhere is a record of what you have purchased, so that it is still there after you change phones. The privacy policy describes exactly what that record contains.

I reinstalled Lupo, or got a new phone.

Purchases you have made but not yet used should still be there. Lupo keeps that record against a random identifier stored in your own iCloud account, so it follows you across a reinstall or a new device.

If you are not signed into iCloud, unused purchases may not carry across a reinstall.

Can I get a refund?

All purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple rather than through me: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Some purchases in Lupo are consumable, and consumables cannot be restored through the App Store once they are used. If something went wrong and cost you one, write to me anyway — I'd like to know about it.
