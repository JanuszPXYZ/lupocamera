Film and purchases

Rolls of film in Lupo are consumable in-app purchases. A roll is used up when you load it into the camera, in the same way a real roll is.

Because consumables are consumed, they cannot be restored through the App Store once spent. Lupo keeps a record of unspent rolls against a random identifier stored in your own iCloud account, so film you have bought but not yet loaded remains available if you reinstall the app or move to a new device. If you are not signed into iCloud, unspent film may not carry across a reinstall.

All purchases are handled by Apple. Refunds are Apple's to give and are requested through Apple, not through us: reportaproblem.apple.com

Your photographs

The photographs you take with Lupo are yours. We claim no rights over them, and we never receive them.

They are stored on your device inside the app. Deleting Lupo deletes them. Rolls you have not saved to your Photos library are not recoverable after that, so save anything you want to keep.
